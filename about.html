Who We Are

The Booking Service Behind the Best Nights Out (or In)

Night Angels started with a simple observation: finding quality party entertainment shouldn't feel like a gamble. Too many guys have booked a "show" off a sketchy ad and ended up with a no-show, a bait-and-switch, or an awkward performance that killed the party instead of making it.

We built Night Angels to be the opposite of that. A real booking service with real coordinators, a vetted roster of professional female entertainers, and a standard for every show that goes out under our name. From the first phone call to the final song, the experience is handled.

Today we book entertainers in major cities across 18+ states — bachelor parties in Vegas and Miami, birthday surprises in Chicago and Atlanta, boat parties in Charleston and San Diego, and everything in between.

Every party gets one shot at being legendary.
We make sure the entertainment isn't the weak link.

Our Standards

What "High Class" Actually Means Here

It's on the logo, so we hold ourselves to it. Every entertainer and every booking runs through the same standards, in every city we serve.

Book With Confidence

Vetted, Experienced Performers

Every entertainer on our roster is interviewed, experienced, and reviewed after bookings. If a performer doesn't hold the standard, she doesn't stay on the roster.

On Time, Every Time

Shows are confirmed in advance and entertainers arrive when scheduled. Your coordinator stays reachable the night of the event in case plans shift.

Discreet Arrivals

Entertainers arrive in regular street clothes and keep things low-key with neighbors, hotel staff, and anyone not on the guest list.

Honest Pricing

The rate quoted on the phone is the rate. No surprise fees at the door, no upsell pressure mid-show. Tips are always at your group's discretion.

Adults-Only, Always

We book private, adults-only events. All guests must be 18 or older — 21+ where required — no exceptions.
